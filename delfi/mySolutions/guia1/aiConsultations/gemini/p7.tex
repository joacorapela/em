I have a charge distribution distributed uniformly along the x axis, from -L/2
to L/2, with a charge density $\lambda$. Is the electric field at position
$(x_0,y_0)$ equal to the following expression?

\begin{align*}
    E((x_0,y_0))=\frac{q}{4\pi\epsilon_0}\left(\left(\frac{1}{\sqrt{(L/2+x_0)^2+y_0^2}}-\frac{1}{\sqrt{(L/2-x_0)^2+y_0^2}}\right)\hat{i}-\right.\\
                                               \quad\left.\frac{1}{y_0}\left(\frac{L/2-x_0}{\sqrt{(L/2-x_0)^2+y_0^2}}-\frac{L/2+x_0}{\sqrt{(L/2+x_0)^2+y_0^2}}\right)\hat{j}\right)
\end{align*}
